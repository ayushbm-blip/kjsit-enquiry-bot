In First Year Engineering at KJSIT, the Semester 1 and Semester 2 subjects are common to all four branches: Computer Engineering, Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, and Electronics and Telecommunication.
In Semester 1, the subjects are: Engineering Mathematics I, Engineering Physics, Engineering Chemistry, Engineering Mechanics, and Basics of Electrical Engineering.
In Semester 1, students also have lab sessions for Engineering Physics, Engineering Chemistry, Engineering Mechanics, and Basics of Electrical Engineering.
In Semester 1, students also take Skill-Based Learning (Workshop-I), a Value Education course (Activity-Based Learning on Contemporary Concerns), and a Co-Curricular course.
In Semester 2, the subjects are: Engineering Mathematics II, Physics and Nanotechnology, Materials Chemistry, Engineering Graphics, and Computer Programming.
In Semester 2, students also have lab sessions for Physics and Nanotechnology, Materials Chemistry, Engineering Graphics, and Computer Programming.
In Semester 2, students also take Professional Communication Skills (Ability Enhancement course), Skill-Based Learning (Workshop-II), and an Indian Knowledge System course.
This is the current First Year curriculum under KJSIT's Autonomy Syllabus Scheme III, aligned with NEP 2020 guidelines, effective from Academic Year 2023-24.
