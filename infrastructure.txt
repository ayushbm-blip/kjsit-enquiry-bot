KJSIT is housed in a G+8 storeyed building, along with the Riturang building, both located at the Ayurvihar campus, Sion.
KJSIT's campus facilities include airy classrooms, hi-tech laboratories, and a library with e-access to digital resources.
KJSIT's campus has Wi-Fi coverage, an auditorium, a seminar hall, an open-air cafeteria, and an amphitheatre.
The campus includes a butterfly garden, developed to make the campus more eco-friendly.
The campus has a ring well used for rainwater harvesting.
KJSIT's campus has both indoor and outdoor sports facilities.
The campus also has an administrative office and common rooms for students.
K. J. Somaiya Hospital, located within the larger Somaiya Ayurvihar complex, is a 550-bed general hospital with a 56-bed super-specialty wing, though it is a separate institution from KJSIT.
KJSIT's campus is part of the larger Somaiya Ayurvihar Complex on the Eastern Express Highway, near Everard Nagar, Sion (East), Mumbai.
