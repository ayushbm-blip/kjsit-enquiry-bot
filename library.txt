The KJSIT Library is called the Knowledge Resource Centre.
The library's collection includes more than 6,500 titles and more than 22,300 volumes.
The library also provides access to about 500 E-journals.
The library's built-up area is approximately 410 square meters.
The library collection covers core engineering fields including Electronics, Computer Science, Information Technology, Telecommunication, Mechanical, Electrical, and Mathematical Sciences, along with books on Social Sciences and general interest.
The library receives about 36 periodicals of professional and general interest.
The KJSIT Library is fully computerized and offers a Web-OPAC (Online Public Access Catalogue) facility.
The library follows an Open Access system, allowing students to pick books themselves, arranged by subject using the Dewey Decimal Classification system.
The library uses Bookworm Library Software for its online catalogue, which shows the rack number of each book.
Students can renew books online through their library account, as long as the book has not been reserved by another reader.
Under the Interlibrary Loan (Global Common Circulation) facility, students can borrow 1 book for 7 days from other Somaiya Institute libraries.
The library's reading hall has a seating capacity of 100 students.
During the regular term, the library is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
During examination periods, the library remains open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and on institute-notified holidays from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Newly admitted students are given a library orientation session to familiarize them with library facilities.
The library also provides an RFID card facility for library access and tracking.
