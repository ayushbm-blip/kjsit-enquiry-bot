KJSIT has a dedicated Training & Placement Cell for student career support.
The Placement Cell's vision is to help every student of KJSIT become a productive and employable engineer.
KJSIT's placement process includes career support opportunities such as Expert Talks by national and international speakers, a Software Development Cell, Interview Guidance, Job Fairs, and a Centre of Excellence.
Other career support opportunities include Internship programs, Robotic Automation exposure, the RIIDL Startup incubator, Grooming Sessions by Alumni, and Joint Placement Drives with other institutes.
KJSIT publishes programme-wise placement statistics and the highest and average salary packages from the last 3 years on its placement page.
Some of the top placement offers at KJSIT have come from IDFC First Bank, with packages around Rs. 13.5 to 13.8 lakh per annum for top-performing students.
For placement-related queries, students and recruiters can contact Mr. Ghanashyam Phadke, Associate Head - Career Management and Corporate Relations.
The Placement Cell can be reached at +91 22 24061412 or via email at placements.tech@somaiya.edu.
The Placement Cell office is located at K. J. Somaiya Institute of Technology, Ground Floor, Somaiya Ayurvihar Complex, Eastern Express Highway, Near Everard Nagar, Sion (East), Mumbai - 400022.
Career guidance responsibility at KJSIT is shared by all Heads of Departments, the placement committee, and various professional bodies.
Seminars and workshops on career guidance are arranged at both the departmental and institute level.
Alumni also visit KJSIT to interact with and mentor current students individually and informally.

Note for verification: exact figures like total number of recruiting companies, current-year highest package, and full recruiter list change every placement season. Please check the live placement page or ask the Training & Placement Cell for this year's exact numbers before the demo.
