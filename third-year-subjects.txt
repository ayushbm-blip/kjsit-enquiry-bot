In Third Year Computer Engineering at KJSIT, Semester 5 subjects are: Computer Network, Machine Learning, Software Engineering, one Department-Level Elective, and a Multidisciplinary Minor Course.
In Semester 5, the Department-Level Elective can be chosen from: Advanced Database Management System, Internet Programming, Web Security, or Probabilistic Graphical Models.
In Semester 5, students also have lab sessions for Computer Network, Machine Learning, and their chosen Department-Level Elective.
In Semester 5, students also work on a Community Engagement Minor Project, a Skill Enhancement course in Aptitude, Logic Building and Competitive Programming, and a Value Education course in Business Communication and Ethics.
In Semester 6, the core subjects are System Programming & Compiler Construction and Deep Learning.
In Semester 6, students choose one Department-Level Elective from: Quantitative Analysis, Multimedia Systems, Infrastructure Security, or Artificial Intelligence and Soft Computing.
In Semester 6, students also take a Multidisciplinary Minor course and one Open Elective (Institute-level), chosen from options like Product Lifecycle Management, Reliability Engineering, Management Information System, Design of Experiments, Operations Research, Cyber Security and Laws, Disaster Management, Energy Audit and Management, or Development Engineering.
In Semester 6, students also work on a Community Engagement Project (Major Project - Part A) and a Cloud Computing skill-based lab.
This is the current Third Year Computer Engineering curriculum under KJSIT's Autonomy Syllabus Scheme III, aligned with NEP 2020 guidelines, effective for Third Year from Academic Year 2025-26.
