In Semester 3 of Second Year Computer Engineering at KJSIT, the complete list of subjects and components is: Applications of Mathematics in Engineering-I, Data Structure (with lab), Database Management System (with lab), Digital Logic & Computer Architecture (with lab), Discrete Structures and Graph Theory, a Community Engagement Mini Project-I, and a Skill Enhancement course in Object Oriented Programming with JAVA. This is the complete and final list of all Semester 3 subjects, labs, and additional components with nothing left out.
In Semester 4 of Second Year Computer Engineering at KJSIT, the complete list of subjects and components is: Applications of Mathematics in Engineering-II, Analysis of Algorithms (with lab), Operating System (with lab), Theory of Computer Science, a Multidisciplinary Minor Course (with lab), a Community Engagement Mini Project-II, a Skill Enhancement course in Python Programming, and an Ability Enhancement course in an Indian or Foreign Modern Language. This is the complete and final list of all Semester 4 subjects, labs, and additional components with nothing left out.
This is the current Second Year Computer Engineering curriculum under KJSIT's Autonomy Syllabus Scheme III, aligned with NEP 2020 guidelines, effective for Second Year from Academic Year 2024-25.
