K J Somaiya Institute of Technology is commonly known as KJSIT.
KJSIT is located at Somaiya Ayurvihar Complex, Eastern Express Highway, Near Everard Nagar, Sion (East), Mumbai - 400022.
KJSIT was established by the Somaiya Trust in the year 2001.
KJSIT is an autonomous institute permanently affiliated to the University of Mumbai.
KJSIT was initiated with a first batch of 180 undergraduate students in three branches: Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, Computer Engineering, and Information Technology.
The Electronics Engineering department was founded in the year 2004.
KJSIT is recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and the Government of Maharashtra.
KJSIT is accredited with an "A" Grade and a CGPA of 3.21 by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) for its first cycle of 5 years.
Three programs at KJSIT are accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA): Computer Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, and Electronics Engineering.
KJSIT has received the "Best College Award" from the University of Mumbai in the urban region category.
KJSIT has also received the "Best Engineering College Award" from the CSI local chapter and from ISTE Maharashtra and Goa Section.
KJSIT introduced a postgraduate engineering program in Artificial Intelligence starting Academic Year 2019-20.
KJSIT introduced an undergraduate engineering program in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science starting Academic Year 2020-21.
The University Grants Commission (UGC) has conferred Autonomous Status on KJSIT for a period of 10 years, from Academic Year 2021-22 to Academic Year 2030-31.
Under this autonomous status, degrees are still awarded by the University of Mumbai.
KJSIT was founded under the vision of Shri Karamshibhai Jethabhai Somaiya, who set up the K J Somaiya Trust in 1951.
The Somaiya Trust runs Somaiya Vidyavihar, a large educational complex with 34 institutions across fields including Engineering, Science, Medicine, Management, and Mass Communication.
Somaiya Vidyavihar has more than 26,000 students and 1,200 teaching faculty across all its institutions.
