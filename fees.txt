The fee structure below is for First Year B.Tech admissions for the academic year 2026-27 at KJSIT.
For the Open category, the tuition fee is Rs. 1,59,565.
For the Open category, the development fee is Rs. 23,935.
For the Open category, the other fee (for Maharashtra State candidates) is Rs. 5,201, bringing the total fee to Rs. 1,88,701.
For the Open category, the other fee (for Outside Maharashtra State candidates) is Rs. 6,021, bringing the total fee to Rs. 1,89,521.
For the OBC/EBC category, the tuition fee is Rs. 79,783, with the same development and other fees as the Open category, bringing the total to Rs. 1,08,919 for Maharashtra State candidates.
For the SC category, the tuition fee and development fee are Rs. 0, with only the other fee of Rs. 5,201 (Maharashtra State) or Rs. 6,021 (Outside Maharashtra State) payable.
For the ST category, the fee structure is the same as the SC category: only the other fee is payable, with tuition and development fees waived.
For VJ, NT, DT, SBC, and SBC-OBC/EBC-Girls categories, the tuition fee is Rs. 0, but the development fee of Rs. 23,935 and other fee of Rs. 5,201 (or Rs. 6,021 for Outside Maharashtra State) are payable, totaling Rs. 29,136 or Rs. 29,956.
For TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) candidates, the tuition fee is Rs. 0, with development and other fees payable, totaling Rs. 29,136 (Maharashtra State) or Rs. 29,956 (Outside Maharashtra State).
For candidates admitted against vacancy seats, the total fee is the same as the Open category: Rs. 1,88,701 (Maharashtra State) or Rs. 1,89,521 (Outside Maharashtra State).
The college notes that fees may change as per guidelines from the Fees Regulating Authority and the University of Mumbai.
Fees can be paid online through net banking, debit card, or credit card.
Fees can also be paid through Demand Draft or Pay Order, made in favor of "K. J. Somaiya Institute of Technology".
Fees can also be paid via NEFT or IMPS, but the institute must be added as a beneficiary at least 24 hours before reporting for counselling.
The bank account for NEFT/IMPS fee payment is held with Kotak Mahindra Bank, Ghatkopar East, Mumbai 400077.
The bank account number for fee payment is 3411560811, and the IFSC code is KKBK0000658.
The full applicable fee must be paid in a single transaction, so candidates should check their bank's transaction limit in advance.
For ACAP (Additional CAP) admissions, no fee concession applies, and the full applicable fee must be paid.
The processing fee for applying to vacant seats (outside the CAP process) is Rs. 1,000.
For the full detailed fee structure document in English, Marathi, and Gujarati, candidates can refer to the official fee structure PDF on the KJSIT admissions page.
