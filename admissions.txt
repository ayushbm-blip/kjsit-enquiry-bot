KJSIT offers four Bachelor of Technology programs: Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, and Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering.
Each of these four B.Tech programs has a sanctioned intake of 120 seats for the 2026-27 academic year.
KJSIT also offers a Master of Technology program in Artificial Intelligence.
KJSIT offers Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) programs in Computer Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, and Information Technology.
Admissions to first year B.Tech are strictly on merit basis, as per rules set by the Directorate of Technical Education and the Admission Regulating Authority, Government of Maharashtra.
For Maharashtra State candidates, eligibility requires passing HSC (12th standard) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, plus one of Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a few other listed subjects.
Maharashtra State candidates need at least 45% marks in the qualifying subjects taken together, or at least 40% for Backward Class, Economically Weaker Section, and Persons with Disability categories.
Maharashtra State candidates must also appear in the CET (Common Entrance Test) and obtain a non-zero score.
For All India candidates, the same HSC eligibility applies, along with a non-zero positive score in JEE (Main) B.E./B.Tech, or a non-zero CET score.
Preference is given to candidates with a JEE (Main) score over those who only have a CET score, for All India seats.
Most seats are filled through CAP (Centralized Admission Process) conducted by the State CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra.
For seats remaining vacant after CAP rounds, online application forms are made available directly on the KJSIT website.
The processing fee for the vacant-seat application form is Rs. 1000.
Candidates applying for vacant seats must have first applied to the Competent Authority through the CAP process for document verification.
Candidates should refer to the KJSIT website for the online application form, prospectus, last date for submissions, merit lists, and counselling schedule.
The Pre-Admission form for 2026-27 can be submitted online between 4th August 2026 and 7th September 2026.
Candidates need their qualifying exam marksheet details (year of passing, percentage, board) ready while filling the Pre-Admission form.
All mandatory fields in the Pre-Admission form must be completed before submission, and information given is treated as final with no later changes allowed.
After submitting the Pre-Admission form, candidates receive an acknowledgement with an "Online application number" and should print two copies of it.
Candidates create an account using their email ID and password, which can also be used to apply to other institutes within Somaiya Vidyavihar.
Documents required for all candidates include: SSC (10th) marksheet, HSC (12th) marksheet, MHT-CET 2026 score card, School Leaving Certificate, and a Certificate of Indian Nationality.
Candidates from outside the Maharashtra State Board also need a Migration Certificate.
A copy of the Aadhar card is required at the time of admission.
Candidates whose HSC passing year is before 2022 need a Gap Affidavit.
Candidates admitted through CAP need to submit their CAP Allotment Letter and Receipt-cum-Acknowledgement from FC/ARC at the time of admission.
All India candidates via JEE Main 2026 Paper-I need to submit their JEE scorecard.
SC/ST candidates need a Caste Certificate, Caste Validity Certificate, Income Certificate, Ration Card, and bank account details, in addition to the general documents.
NT1, NT2, NT3, OBC, and SBC candidates need a Caste Certificate, Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (valid until 31st March 2026), Caste Validity Certificate, Income Certificate, Ration Card, and bank account details.
For admission queries, candidates can contact enquiry.tech@somaiya.edu.
Candidates can apply online at admissions.somaiya.edu.
In addition to the general documents (SSC marksheet, HSC marksheet, MHT-CET score card, School Leaving Certificate, Certificate of Indian Nationality, and Migration Certificate if applicable), some candidates need extra documents: a copy of the Aadhar card is required from all candidates at admission time; candidates whose HSC passing year is before 2022 need a Gap Affidavit; SC/ST candidates need a Caste Certificate, Caste Validity Certificate, Income Certificate, Ration Card, and bank account details; NT/OBC/SBC candidates need a Caste Certificate, Non-Creamy Layer Certificate, Caste Validity Certificate, Income Certificate, Ration Card, and bank account details; candidates admitted through CAP need their CAP Allotment Letter and Receipt-cum-Acknowledgement from FC/ARC; and All India candidates via JEE Main need to submit their JEE scorecard.
