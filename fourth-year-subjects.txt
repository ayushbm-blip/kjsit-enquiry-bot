In Last Year (Fourth Year) Computer Engineering at KJSIT, Semester 7 subjects are: Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, two Department-Level Electives, and one Institute-Level Elective.
In Semester 7, the Department-Level Electives can be chosen from: Computer Vision, Adhoc Wireless Network, Ethical Hacking and Security, Natural Language Processing, Augmented and Virtual Reality, Internet Communication, Blockchain, or Information Retrieval.
In Semester 7, the Institute-Level Elective can be chosen from: Product Lifecycle Management, Reliability Engineering, Management Information System, Design of Experiments, Operation Research, Cyber Security and Laws, Disaster Management and Mitigation Measures, Energy Audit and Management, or Development Engineering.
In Semester 7, students also have lab sessions for Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, and one Department-Level Elective, plus a Major Project Part A and a 2 to 4 week Internship.
In Semester 8, subjects are: Distributed Computing, two Department-Level Electives, and one Institute-Level Elective.
In Semester 8, the Department-Level Electives can be chosen from: Applied Data Science, Software Defined Network, Digital Forensics, Deep Learning, Social Media Analytics, High Performance Computing, Secure Application Development, Optimization in Machine Learning, or Quantum Computing.
In Semester 8, the Institute-Level Elective can be chosen from: Project Management, Finance Management, Entrepreneurship Development and Management, Human Resource Management, Professional Ethics and CSR, Research Methodology, IPR and Patenting, Digital Business Management, or Environmental Management.
In Semester 8, students also have lab sessions for Distributed Computing and their two Department-Level Electives, plus a Major Project Part B and a further Internship.
This is the current Last Year (Fourth Year) Computer Engineering curriculum under KJSIT's Autonomy Syllabus Scheme III, aligned with NEP 2020 guidelines, effective for Last Year from Academic Year 2026-27.
